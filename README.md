\# Relativistic Antimatter Confinement \& Annihilation Energy Modeling



\[!\[License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

\[!\[Python: 3.10+](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.10%2B-blue)](https://www.python.org/)



A numerical simulation framework in Python studying the theoretical feasibility of antimatter ($p\\bar{p}$) as an ultra-high energy density storage medium.



\## Key Features

1\. \*\*Relativistic Particle-in-Cell Boris Pusher:\*\* Simulates antiproton trajectories in a Penning-Malmberg trap under coupled quadrupolar electrostatic and axial magnetic fields.

2\. \*\*Monte Carlo Annihilation Cascades:\*\* Simulates $p\\bar{p} \\to \\pi^\\pm + \\pi^0$ branching ratios and kinetic energy partitioning for direct Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) conversion.
